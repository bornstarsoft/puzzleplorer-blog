The Daily Mail has just launched Relink, a captivating new puzzle game that challenges your lateral thinking and problem-solving skills. With its unique format of identifying imposters among a grid of words, Relink offers a refreshing twist on traditional brainteasers, making it a must-try for puzzle enthusiasts and casual gamers alike.

Each day, players are presented with a grid of 16 words, where three share a common link while one is an imposter. The thrill lies in not just identifying the imposters but also uncovering the hidden connection that ties them together. With only four lives to spare, players must think critically and strategically to solve the puzzle before the day resets.

As Relink goes live every day at midnight, it encourages a daily ritual of mental exercise and friendly competition among friends. Will you be the first to crack the code and keep your streak alive? Dive into Relink and discover a new way to engage your mind!
